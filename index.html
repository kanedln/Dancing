Questions

Before you book

Do I need to be home?

No. Most clients leave a lockbox code, smart-lock PIN or key with the office. We confirm arrival and send finish photos when we’re done.

Do you cover my town?

We cover Sheboygan and Manitowoc counties — Sheboygan, Sheboygan Falls, Kohler, Plymouth, Oostburg, Cedar Grove, Elkhart Lake, Manitowoc, Two Rivers, Kiel, Mishicot, Valders and the towns between. Just outside that? Call anyway; we'll tell you straight whether we can get there.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Everything — equipment, cloths and products. We carry eco and fragrance-free lines too; just flag it on the quote form.

How fast can you turn an Airbnb?

Standard flips are same-day with a confirmed window. Recurring hosts get priority slots and back-to-back checkout coverage.

Are you insured and background-checked?

Yes — fully insured and bonded, and every cleaner passes a background check. Certificates of insurance go out to property managers on request.

What if I’m not happy with the clean?

Tell us within 24 hours and we return and re-clean the area free. No invoice games, no arguing over what “clean” means.

How do I pay?

Card, ACH or invoice for property managers and brokerages. Payment is due after the clean, not before.
